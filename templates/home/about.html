About Our Placement Portal

Our Campus Placement Portal is designed to bridge the gap between academic education and corporate careers. We provide an end-to-end recruitment management system that empowers students to showcase their academic histories and skillsets, companies to discover exceptional candidates, and placement departments to track progress.

Key Features

  • Automated applicant eligibility verification based on CGPA criteria.
  • Live tracking of application status (Applied, Shortlisted, Selected, Rejected).
  • Comprehensive interview scheduling workflow.
  • In-app alert notifications for students and company representatives.
  • Aggregated analytics and CSV report generation for university administrators.

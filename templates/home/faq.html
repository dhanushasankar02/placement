Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How do I apply for jobs on the portal?

A: First, register as a Student and complete your profile by uploading a resume and filling in your CGPA. Browse the active job lists and click 'Apply' for any job you are eligible for.

Q: Why does the system say I am not eligible to apply?

A: Each job posted has a minimum eligibility criteria (CGPA). If your profile CGPA is lower than the job's requirements, the apply button will be locked for quality assurance.

Q: How are interviews scheduled?

A: Once a company shortlists your application, they schedule an interview round. You will receive an in-app notification with the date and links/location for the interview.
